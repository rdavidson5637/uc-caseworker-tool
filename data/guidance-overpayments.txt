BENEFIT OVERPAYMENTS (INCLUDING UNIVERSAL CREDIT)
Source: gov.uk/benefit-overpayments (retrieved 7 August 2026)
Note: the brief's original URL gov.uk/universal-credit/overpayments now redirects; this is the current GOV.UK overpayments guidance.

OVERVIEW

Tell the office dealing with your benefit straight away if:
- you think you are being overpaid
- your circumstances change

You may have to pay back the benefit if you have been overpaid.

You may be prosecuted for benefit fraud or have to pay a penalty if you do not tell benefit providers about overpayments.

IF YOU HAVE BEEN OVERPAID UNIVERSAL CREDIT

You can report an overpayment by signing into your Universal Credit account or calling the Universal Credit helpline.

Universal Credit helpline: 0800 328 5644
Welsh language: 0800 328 1744
Relay UK (if you cannot hear or speak on the phone): 18001 then 0800 328 5644
British Sign Language (BSL) video relay service is available.
Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm.

WHEN THE BENEFIT OFFICE WILL CONTACT YOU

You will get a letter to let you know that you have been overpaid. If you think it is a mistake you can ask for mandatory reconsideration. You usually need to do this within one month of receiving the letter.

WHEN REPAYMENTS HAVE TO BE MADE

You may have to pay the money back if you have been overpaid. For example, if:
- the information you gave was wrong
- you did not report a change in your circumstances straight away
- you gave the wrong information when you reported a change of circumstances
- a mistake was made with your payment

There is a different system if the person overpaid has died.

HOW TO MAKE A REPAYMENT

How you pay back the overpayment depends on whether you are making repayments for the first time or restarting them, and whether you still receive benefits.

If you are still receiving benefits: the regular amount you get will be reduced until you have paid back the money. Contact the DWP Debt Management contact centre if you think too much has been taken for a repayment.

If you no longer receive benefits: you will get a letter from DWP Debt Management explaining how to repay and manage benefit money you owe. You can pay back the overpayment in full or set up regular monthly payments.

GET HELP WITH YOUR REPAYMENTS

Contact DWP Debt Management if you need help managing your repayments. They can talk you through your options, including what you can afford to pay.

DWP Debt Management contact centre: 0800 916 0647
Relay UK: 18001 then 0800 916 0647
Calling from abroad: +44 (0)161 904 1233
Monday to Friday, 8am to 7:30pm.

IF YOU DO NOT PAY BACK THE MONEY

If you do not pay back the money or contact DWP Debt Management, they may:
- ask your employer to make deductions from your pay (Direct Earnings Attachment)
- pass your case to an independent debt collector (Advantis, BPO Collections, CCS Collect, Moorcroft, Past Due Credit, Resolve Call, or Shakespeare Martineau)
- pass your case to the DWP Debt Enforcement Team

If your case is passed to the Debt Enforcement Team, you will be asked to set up a repayment plan. If you do not contact the team or do not follow your repayment plan, the team will submit a claim against you to HM Courts and Tribunals Service and add extra costs to the money you owe. You will then need to repay all the money you owe within 6 months or the team will apply for a county court judgment. A county court judgment adds further costs and affects your credit score for up to 6 years.
